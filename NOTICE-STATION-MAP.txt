CoachAnnouncer packages the selected BusStop and viewpoint identifiers and
world X/Z coordinates from busStations, busTouristStops and viewpoints as TSV.
The rest of the map export is omitted. See TRUCKDASH-LICENSE.txt for the
upstream project's AGPL-3.0 license text. The corresponding upstream file and
source are available at the URL above. The converter is supplied under
source/CoachAnnouncer/tools/Import-Station-Map.ps1.
